Paper machine: AMD Threadripper 9970X, 32 cores, AVX-512; all-cores rows use 32 threads, one per core.
Time: each step's time / the paper form's time, at the paper's sizes on all cores.
* Fusion only recomputes the recurrence from its start for every output, O(T^2), so it is
  measured at a small size on one thread, against the paper form at that same size.
= (2): the paper form does not unroll this walk, so step (3) is the step (2) schedule.
